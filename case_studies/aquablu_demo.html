What just happened

    Why AURA suggests these quantities — rules and assumptions

    Prototype choices: seven days of safety stock; target covers shipping plus fourteen days. These are not established Aquablu rules. The suggested one-box Citrus order does not account for minimum order quantities, shipping costs or order consolidation. Rates use observed flavour days only; missing rows remain unknown. Calendar shipping days assumed. Active-box liquid excluded.

    Count establishes stock. Receipt adds boxes; delivery alone does not. Confirmed fresh installation removes one, unlike a raw hardware connection. At or below reorder point, suggest replenishment. Rejection retains stock risk. Unavailable Berry stays unmet and is excluded from new orders. This does not resolve existing backorders or guarantee shipment.
