Personal product / Built with Lovable

BeltPath

I turned my Lean Six Sigma study needs into a working learning workspace.

The need

I wanted a place to work through Green Belt material, practise applying it and keep track of what I had studied. BeltPath brings those steps into one browser-based workspace.

What I built with AI

I defined the study need and used Lovable to turn it into a working application. Lovable generated the implementation; I supplied the purpose and direction.

The learning workflow

  1. Open a chapter and work through the material.
  2. Try diagnostic questions and calculation labs before revealing solutions.
  3. Use the searchable reference when a method needs clarification.
  4. Take a timed mock and review the explanations.
  5. Return to the roadmap and record progress.

What is available

The current application includes ten chapters, twelve practice labs, a 110-question mock and an eight-week study roadmap.

A deliberate boundary: local progress

Progress is stored in the current browser, without an account or cloud sync. That keeps the study flow simple, but clearing browser data can remove progress. Export and import controls let the learner move a backup between devices.

What still needs validation

A working study tool does not prove learning effectiveness. I would assess whether practice and review improve understanding, validate the content with qualified reviewers, and test whether progress recovery is clear enough for learners.

Where it stands

BeltPath is a working personal learning product supporting my Green Belt preparation. It does not award certification and is not affiliated with or endorsed by ASQ.
